About Arts Access Miami

Uniting Miami through the power of arts education. Arts Access unites a network of grantee organizations to bring free, high-quality music, dance, theater, and visual arts instruction to K-12 students across Miami-Dade County — replacing competition with collaboration to reach every child. This portal is where our grantee partners manage reporting deadlines, coordinate events, and stay connected with one another.
